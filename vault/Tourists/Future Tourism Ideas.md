# Future Tourism Ideas

**Breadcrumb**: Tourists > Future Tourism Ideas  
**TL;DR**: Parked product ideas — not scheduled for 0.9 or v1.0 unless promoted from `tasks/todo.md`.

## Ideas (not implemented)

- **Create Aeronautics** — treat planes, balloons, and similar Create (or addon) vehicles as another supported transport mode for tourists, with the same distance / expiry rules as minecarts and trains.
- **Elevation-aware rewards** — include net elevation change (or max altitude) when calculating tourist payouts or milestone bonuses so mountain routes pay differently than flat deserts.
- **Achievements** — e.g. **Flying High** for carrying a tourist above a configured Y level or altitude band.
- **Extreme speed bonuses** — optional bonus emeralds when a tourist completes a leg faster than Create trains’ configured max speed (needs clear measurement and anti-cheat).
