# Town Overview

**TL;DR**: A town is registered when a player places one **Town Centre** block — it has a name, centre position, population, resources, payment board, up to ten **Platform Interface**–linked platforms, visit history, and a fixed border radius (not population-grown). Tourism grows population, which unlocks capacity and upgrades. There is no town owner.

## Processes in this area
- **[[Town/Resources/Resource Storage Operations|Resource Storage Operations]]** (T-007) — Every town keeps a ledger of resources (wood, food, emeralds, anything). Adding and spending is safe by design: counts can never overflow or go negative, and everything survives save/reload.
- **[[Town/Resources/Work Unit Accounting|Work Unit Accounting]]** (T-035) — Work units are a virtual internal counter that production recipes consume as costs and produce as yields. Adds and sets are protected from negatives and overflow; an upgrade modifier can impose an upper cap.
- **[[Town/Trading/Stock and Capacity Resolution|Stock and Capacity Resolution]]** (T-031) — Everything that needs to know "how much does this town have?" — production, research decisions, trading — reads through one consistent view that covers real items and also virtual quantities like population, active tourists, and happiness. Storage limits grow with town upgrades.
- **[[Town/Visits/Visit Buffer|Visit Buffer]]** (T-010) — Tourists arriving around the same time from the same origin town are grouped into one batch (about a 1-second window), so the town gets one combined payment and one notification instead of spam.
- **[[Town/Visits/Visit History Recording|Visit History Recording]]** (T-039) — Towns keep a rolling log of recent tourist group arrivals (which origin, how many together, rough starting location) plus simple running totals for all tourists received and a summed straight-line "distance traveled by visitors" figure; the log feeds the visitor history UI while the totals support stats and leaderboards.
- **[[Town/Leaderboard/Ranking Calculation|Ranking Calculation]]** (T-011) — The leaderboard screen ranks towns by distance from you, population, visits, or total tourism, and formats distances nicely (meters under 1 km, kilometers above).
- **[[Town/Payment Board/Reward Claims|Reward Claims]]** (T-012) — All town income (tourist fares, milestone bonuses, trade profits, courier payouts) lands on a payment board as claimable entries. Some are open to everyone, others reserved for a specific player (e.g. the courier who did the work). Entries expire after 7 days and the board keeps at most 100.
- **[[Town/Boundaries/Town Distance Validation|Town Distance Validation]]** (T-008) — Towns can't be founded too close to each other. Each town's boundary radius comes from a border modifier or config default (50 blocks), and a new Town Centre must be at least `newRadius + existingRadius` from any other town. Town names are also validated here (length, characters, appropriateness).
- **[[Town/Boundaries/Boundary Geometry Queries|Boundary Geometry Queries]]** (T-036) — Every town knows its own protected radius (50 blocks for a fresh settlement, or whatever an upgrade has set) and can say whether a world position falls inside it or whether its circle would touch another town's circle.
- **[[Town/Storage/Slot-Based Storage|Slot-Based Storage]]** (T-013) — A chest-like grid storage used by the payment board's claim buffer (18 slots). Items stack into matching slots first, then empty ones, exactly like you'd expect from a chest.
- **[[Town/Platforms/Platform Interface|Platform Interface]]** — Players add platforms by placing Platform Interface blocks inside the boundary (cap 10, min 5-block separation). Path, destinations, search radius, spread, and enable/disable are edited on the block; the Town Centre platform tab is read-only.
- **[[Town/Platforms/Platform Data Model|Platform Data Model]]** (T-015) — Each platform stores its interface block position, path endpoints, search radius, spread, destination allow-list, and enabled flag; spawning and lamps respect enabled + complete paths.
- **[[Town/Platforms/Platform Management|Platform Management]]** (T-023) — `PlatformManager` holds the list: `addInterfacePlatform` / `relink` from interface blocks, cap and separation rules, client snapshot sync, NBT save/load (entries without an interface position are dropped).
- **[[Town/Decisions/Town Centre and Platform Decisions|Town Centre and Platform Decisions]]** — 2026-10-26 alignment: Town Centre registration, no legacy platform migration, tourists never walk, subsystem defaults, CI manual-only.
- **[[Tourists/Tourists Overview|Tourist Capacity & Allocation]]** (T-009) — How many tourists a town can send depends on its population, and destinations are chosen fairly so bigger towns receive proportionally more visitors.
- **[[Town/Data Synchronization/Container Data Registration|Container Data Registration]]** (T-024) — The Town Interface menu always shows live town values (population, tourist counts, boundary size) because every number on screen is wired by name to the real value, with protection so the screen can never accidentally change something it should only display.
- **[[Town/Data Synchronization/Client Data Synchronization|Client Data Synchronization]]** (T-033) — What the player sees in town screens (resources, storage, visit history) is a local copy kept up to date from the server, and visitor town names are worked out ahead of time so lists and tooltips appear instantly instead of waiting on lookups.
- **[[Town/Production/Recipe Execution and Dynamic Evaluation|Recipe Execution and Dynamic Evaluation]]** (T-029) — Production recipes (population growth, work units, farming, tourist generation) run automatically on a progress clock; output amounts scale with the town's current population, happiness, and upgrades, recipes pause when storage is full or their conditions aren't met, and a food shortage slowly shrinks the population.
- **[[Town/Contracts/Autonomous Bidding and Sell Contract Creation|Autonomous Bidding and Sell Contract Creation]]** (T-034) — Towns trade on their own: when a town piles up far more of a resource than it needs, it sets the surplus aside and offers it for sale at a price near the going market rate (bigger surpluses get discounted more). Towns also watch the market and bid on goods they are short of, with sensible budgets that include delivery costs, a limit of three open deals at a time, and a rule against bidding on something they are also selling.
- **Biome kits at founding** (T-041) — When a town is created, its biome kit can grant starting population, happiness, pending tourists, border/capacity modifiers, research unlocks, and resource stock. Feature toggles filter that kit: research off keeps only the basic settlement unlock (so tourism still works), and production off skips starting food/wood/money. Details: [[Config/Subsystem Toggle Honesty]].

## How it connects
A town is born when a player places a **Town Centre** block. Platform Interface blocks inside the border define where tourists spawn and which destinations are allowed. Everything else loops through resources and population: tourists generate fares (→ payment board), fares and (when enabled) trade fill the ledger, production and upgrades consume resources, and population growth raises tourist capacity. Boundaries use configured radius plus separation from other towns; visit history records arrivals.
